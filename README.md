# Noor & Mahabba Event System (1446 AH)

An illuminated, responsive digital architecture commemorating the sacred nocturnal gathering and life of the Prophet of Mercy (*Rahmatan lil-’Alamin*). Built with the **Sacred Emerald & Antique Gold** design system: a spiritually reverent, regal illuminated manuscript aesthetic marrying Ottoman-Persian arabesque filigree and botanical tulip adornments with contemporary high-precision dark glassmorphic UI.

---

## 🎨 Theme & Design System: Sacred Emerald & Antique Gold

### Aesthetic Persona & Tone
- **Sacred Luxury & Glassmorphic Manuscript**: Richly textured emerald velvet canvases (`#0D2B1D`), gilded filigree frames, luminous gold leaf accents (`#D4AF37`), and lifelike botanical tulip adornments evoking traditional Ottoman illumination (*tezhip*).
- **Target Experience**: An heirloom liturgical manuscript brought into the digital age for illuminated spiritual gatherings, recitations, and sacred symposiums.
- **Key Visual Pillars**: Gilded filigree borders, illuminated gold foil typography, deep emerald depth layers, warm candlelight glows, and scarlet botanical accents.

### Colors & Materials
- **Canvas Base (`#14140b` / `#0D2B1D`)**: Deepest textured sanctuary emerald velvet graded with central radial candlelight illumination.
- **Glassmorphic Depth**: Level 1 frosted panels (`rgba(20, 61, 43, 0.70)`, 12px blur, gold hairline), Level 2 elevated containers (`rgba(27, 77, 54, 0.85)` with gold double border), and Level 3 floating golden halos (`0 0 24px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.40)`).
- **Dynamic Gold Spectrum**: Antique gold (`#D4AF37`), pale highlight (`#F3E5AB`), gold foil (`#E8CA65`), and engraved deep bronze (`#997A15`).
- **Ottoman Crimson / Tulip Red (`#C0262D`)**: High-priority badges, live status indicators, and botanical accents.
- **Parchment Ivory (`#F7F3E3` / `#E6E3D3`)**: Crisp, warm reading typography over dark emerald surfaces.

### Typography Engine
- **Sacred Arabic Epigraph Tier**: `Amiri` (*وَمَا أَرْسَلْنَاكَ إِلَّا رَحْمَةً لِّلْعَالَمِينَ*), bold, generously sized, with golden illumination shadow.
- **Regal Inscriptional Display Tier**: `Cinzel` (500, 600, 700, 800, 900) for monumental western titles, gilded hero headings, and ceremonial inscriptions.
- **Contemporary Interface & Body Tier**: `Outfit` (300, 400, 500, 600, 700) for crisp, effortless readability across dark emerald glass, agendas, articles, and high-density telemetry.

## 📄 Core Modules & Features

1. **Home Landing & Invocations**:
   - Radiant hero section with Arabic calligraphy epigraph.
   - Live KPI statistics strip.
   - Editorial Dispatches feed and Editor's Choice spotlight paper.
   - Sacred divider motifs with miniature gold diamonds (`◆ ✧ ◆`).

2. **Visual Arts & Exhibition Gallery**:
   - High-resolution gallery grid with category filters (`Stage Programs`, `Exhibitions`, `Awards`).
   - Real-time search, category badges, and full Lightbox illumination modal with media download.

3. **Dispatches & Press Bulletins**:
   - Official press releases with date badges, reading estimates, and full-story reader modal.

4. **Student Academic Journal**:
   - Peer-reviewed research essays with author profiles, abstracts, `A-` / `A+` font controls, and print/PDF support.

5. **Interactive Seerah Challenge & Leaderboard**:
   - Chronometer telemetry in gold monospaced tabular numerals.
   - Step-by-step questions with immediate historical context and explanations.
   - High-density telemetry Leaderboard with Top-3 podium medals (`🥇`, `🥈`, `🥉`).
   - **Dynamic Certificate Canvas**: Authoritative parchment-inspired plate bordered by an ornate double gold ribbon hairline, integrated Quranic calligraphy watermark (*Rahmatan lil-’Alamin*), dynamic user typography, and illuminated gold-foil circular seal (PNG download).

6. **Admin Control Center (PIN: `1446`)**:
   - Complete administrative CRUD management for gallery folios, news dispatches, research papers, and challenge questions, with JSON backup export/import and factory reset.
